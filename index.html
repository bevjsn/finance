What am I looking at?

Markets don't return the same amount every year. This runs 500 simulated futures for your portfolio, each year drawing a random return from a bell curve centered on your expected return (currently 7%) with ±14% volatility — so some years boom, some crash.

Each line is a percentile of where you end up across all 500 runs. P50 (the bold line) is the median — half the outcomes land above it, half below. The shaded band spans P10 to P90: a 10% chance you do worse than the bottom edge, a 10% chance you do better than the top. A wide band means more uncertainty.

Contributions and loan payoff are applied each year; cash grows at a steady 4.5%. This is a projection, not a guarantee.
